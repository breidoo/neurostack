    Coils

    Suggested sequence: one framing coil, then filling coils of decreasing loop diameter, then finishing coils. Change any loop diameter, length or wire diameter, remove a coil or add one; the packing density follows.

    How this is calculated and sources
    • Aneurysm volume = π/6 × width (projection 1) × width (projection 2) × height (average of the two projections).
    • Coil volume = π × (wire diameter / 2)² × length. Packing density = total coil volume / aneurysm volume × 100.
    • Suggested sequence (rule of thumb): framing coil with a loop diameter equal to the mean of the three dimensions, rounded to 0.5 mm, and 3 cm of length per mm of loop diameter; filling coils stepping down 1 mm at a time to half the framing diameter, 2.5 cm per mm; finishing coils at that smallest diameter, 2 cm per mm; coils are added until the planned packing density is reached.
    • Packing density definition and the 24% figure: Sluzewski et al., Radiology 2004; Slob et al., AJNR 2005 (ajnr.org/node/1913).
    • Wire diameter and packing: PMC7977130. Packing density is not the only factor in recanalization: PMC4859523.
    Disclaimer

    Estimate only. This does not replace the coil manufacturer's IFU or clinical judgement. The coils here are generic: real coils come in fixed loop diameters and lengths, and their wire diameter differs by product line and softness. The suggested sequence is a rule of thumb, not a manufacturer recommendation. The aneurysm volume is that of an ellipsoid, which can differ considerably from the true volume of an irregular aneurysm.

    SIH (spontaneous intracranial hypotension)

    How to measure

    MRI examples of all six signs: Figure 2 in Dobrocky et al., JAMA Neurology 2019. The drawings below are schematic.

    1. Suprasellar cistern, 4 mm or less: the height of the cistern above the sella on a sagittal image, between the optic chiasm and the sella.
    2. Mamillopontine distance, 6.5 mm or less: from the inferior aspect of the mamillary bodies to the superior aspect of the pons, on the midline sagittal image.
    3. Prepontine cistern, 5 mm or less: between the ventral surface of the pons and the clivus on a sagittal image.

    Pachymeningeal enhancement: smooth, diffuse enhancement of the dura on T1-weighted images after contrast.

    Engorgement of venous sinuses: a distended dural venous sinus, for example a convex border of the dominant transverse sinus.

    Subdural fluid collection: present or absent (hygroma or haematoma); no size criterion.

    Simplified drawings made for this tool, not patient images; not to scale.

    Sources
    • Bern score: Dobrocky et al., JAMA Neurol 2019;76:580–587. Major criteria, 2 points each: pachymeningeal enhancement, engorgement of the venous sinus, suprasellar cistern of 4.0 mm or less. Minor criteria, 1 point each: subdural fluid collection, prepontine cistern of 5.0 mm or less, mamillopontine distance of 6.5 mm or less. 2 points or fewer: low probability of a spinal CSF leak; 3 to 4: intermediate; 5 or more: high.
    • Mamillopontine distance landmarks: Shah et al., AJR 2013;200:400–407. The measuring guide is a drawing made for this tool, not an image from a paper.
